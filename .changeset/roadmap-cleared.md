Density, small multiples and chart textures. `data-density="compact"` on any ancestor tightens control heights (28/32/40, Button LG 44) and table rows, with a Storybook toolbar to try it. New `SmallMultiples` (one area chart per category, shared y-scale by default). `texture` on `StackedBarChart`, `DonutChart` and `Legend` adds a fixed pattern per series for print, greyscale and colour-blind readers.
